# Privacy Policy

**Policy version: 2026-11-14**

## 1. Introduction

Radix Minds respects the privacy of students, parents, guardians and visitors who use our website and educational services.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored, processed and shared when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, register for a program, participate in classes or contact our support team.

Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain it through an appropriate process.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services used, we may collect the following information.

### A. Student information

- Student's name.
- Class or academic level.
- Selected academic pathway.
- School board.
- Preferred language.
- Contact information.
- Selected learning mode.
- Enrolment reference and program details.

### B. Enquiry and communication information

- Messages and questions submitted through our website.
- Contact and support history.
- Communication preferences.

### C. Enrolment and transaction information

Where applicable:

- Selected program and learning mode.
- Program fee and payable amount.
- Enrolment status.
- Payment status.
- Transaction references and payment identifiers.
- Refund and cancellation request details.

Payment card numbers, UPI PINs, banking passwords and payment authentication credentials should be entered only through the authorised payment provider's interface. Radix Minds does not intend to collect or store those credentials directly.

### D. Technical information

Our website and service providers may process:

- IP address.
- Browser and device information.
- Operating system.
- Access timestamps.
- Website interaction and error information.
- Security and diagnostic logs.

Essential website technologies may be used for security, navigation and functionality. Optional analytics or marketing technologies, if introduced, will be subject to appropriate notice and consent arrangements where required.

## 3. Why We Collect Information

We may use personal information to:

1. Respond to enquiries.
2. Process registration and enrolment requests.
3. Communicate program and batch information.
4. Provide access to selected learning modes.
5. Manage academic and administrative records.
6. Provide student support.
7. Maintain transaction and refund records.
8. Prevent fraud and unauthorised activity.
9. Maintain website security and performance.
10. Comply with applicable legal requirements.

We will not knowingly use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis or consent where required.

## 4. Children and Child-Data Protection

Our programs accept students below 18 years of age and students aged 18 years and above.

When processing personal information relating to a student below 18, Radix Minds will apply safeguards applicable to children's personal data under the laws in force. We do not intend to use children's personal information for targeted advertising or unrelated commercial profiling.

Students and their authorised representatives may contact Radix Minds with questions or requests concerning a student's information. Applicable legal requirements relating to children's personal data remain in force.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We may share relevant information with authorised service providers where necessary to operate our services, including:

- Website and application hosting providers.
- Database and infrastructure providers.
- Communication and email service providers.
- Learning delivery platforms.
- Payment service providers, when payment integration is enabled.
- Professional advisers or authorities where disclosure is legally required.

We aim to share only information necessary for the relevant purpose.

We do not sell student personal information as a commercial product.

## 6. Website, Database and Payment Service Providers

Radix Minds uses third-party service providers to operate the bootcamp website, application, enrolment records and online payments.

- **Netlify** hosts and deploys the Radix Minds 75-Day Board Bootcamp website and application, including its serverless application functions.
- **Supabase** provides the application's database and authentication services used to manage enrolment and authorised administrative access.
- **Razorpay** processes online payment transactions when checkout is enabled. Radix Minds receives the transaction references, payment status and related payment information needed to manage enrolments and refunds.

These providers may process information necessary to deliver, secure and maintain their respective services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Payment Information

When online payments are enabled, payment transactions may be processed through a third-party payment gateway.

The payment provider may collect and process payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Radix Minds may receive transaction references, payment status, payment amount and related information required to confirm enrolment or process refunds.

We do not intend to store full card numbers, UPI PINs, banking passwords or payment authentication credentials on our own servers.

## 8. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or misuse.

Depending on the service and information involved, these measures may include:

- Access controls.
- Role-based administrative permissions.
- Secure server-side database access.
- Authentication and authorisation controls.
- HTTPS communication.
- Restricted access to sensitive records.
- Security monitoring and maintenance.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. We will take reasonable steps to investigate and address suspected security incidents.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including enrolment administration, academic support, financial records, legal compliance, dispute resolution and security.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Specific retention periods will be established for relevant categories of information.

## 10. Requests and Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, students and their authorised representatives may request:

- Access to relevant personal information.
- Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Erasure where legally applicable.
- Withdrawal of consent where applicable.
- Information about the handling of a privacy concern.
- Other rights available under applicable data protection law.

Requests may be submitted using the contact details below.

We may need to verify the identity and authority of the person making a request.

## 11. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use essential technologies required for functionality, navigation, session management and security.

If optional analytics, advertising or tracking technologies are introduced, appropriate notices and consent arrangements will be provided where required.

Users may also control cookies through their browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

## 12. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms.

Radix Minds is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of independent third-party websites. Users should review those services' privacy policies before sharing information with them.
