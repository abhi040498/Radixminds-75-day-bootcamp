# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## 1. Introduction

Radix Minds aims to provide students with a clear and transparent enrolment and cancellation process.

This policy explains how a student may request cancellation, withdrawal or a refund for the 75-Day Board Preparation Bootcamp.

It applies to Class 10 and Class 12 and all three learning modes:

- Online – Live Interactive Classes.
- Hybrid – Flexible Online + Classroom Learning.
- Offline – Classroom Training.

## 2. General Refund Principles

Refund requests will be reviewed fairly and in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

A student or their authorised representative may contact Radix Minds to request cancellation or a refund.

A request will not be rejected solely because it was submitted through the wrong communication channel, provided that the student's identity and enrolment can reasonably be verified.

The refund request process is:

1. The customer contacts the Radix Minds team by support email or phone.
2. The team reviews the request.
3. The team communicates its decision directly to the customer.
4. If the refund is approved, the team initiates it. No separate management approval or additional approval stage is required.
5. The target processing time is 7–10 working days from approval, subject to bank and payment-provider timelines.

## 3. Cancellation Before the Program Begins

A student who requests cancellation before the scheduled program commencement date will be eligible for a full refund of the program fee actually received.

This applies to Online, Hybrid and Offline enrolments.

The request should include:

- Student's full name.
- Enrolment reference.
- Registered mobile number or email address.
- Reason for cancellation, if the student wishes to provide one.

## 4. Withdrawal After the Program Begins

### A. Withdrawal within the first seven calendar days

A student who requests withdrawal within the first seven calendar days from the program commencement date may be eligible for a prorated refund.

The precise refund calculation and any applicable adjustment for the portion of the program already delivered remain subject to discussion. No calculation has been agreed.

Any final calculation method will be published or communicated clearly before enrolment terms are finalised.

No arbitrary or undisclosed administration deduction will be applied.

### B. Withdrawal after the first seven calendar days

After the initial seven-day period, voluntary withdrawal will generally not qualify for an automatic refund.

However, Radix Minds may review exceptional circumstances, including serious medical situations, unforeseen events or other reasonable grounds.

Any decision will be communicated to the student or authorised representative with an explanation.

Nothing in this clause removes any refund or consumer rights available under applicable law.

## 5. Cancellation by Radix Minds

If Radix Minds cancels a batch or is unable to deliver the advertised program and cannot offer a suitable alternative accepted by the student, the student will be eligible for a full refund of the amount paid for the undelivered program.

Radix Minds will communicate the cancellation and refund procedure to affected students.

## 6. Material Changes to the Program

Where a material change substantially affects the advertised program, Radix Minds will inform enrolled students.

Where an acceptable alternative cannot be agreed upon, the affected student may request cancellation and an appropriate refund for the undelivered portion of the service, subject to applicable law.

Minor operational changes that do not materially affect the academic program will not automatically qualify for a refund.

## 7. Failed, Duplicate and Unsuccessful Payments

### A. Failed payment

If a payment attempt fails but the student's bank account or payment instrument is debited, the transaction will be checked against the payment provider and banking records.

Any amount confirmed as received by or payable to Radix Minds without a corresponding valid enrolment will be refunded or otherwise resolved appropriately.

### B. Duplicate payment

If a student is charged more than once for the same enrolment, the duplicate amount will be refunded after verification.

### C. Payment received but enrolment not confirmed

If payment is successfully received but the enrolment is not confirmed due to a technical or administrative issue, Radix Minds will investigate and either complete the enrolment or process an appropriate refund.

## 8. Learning Mode Changes

Students may request a change between Online, Hybrid and Offline learning, subject to batch capacity and operational availability.

All three modes provide the same academic structure and program benefits.

A learning-mode change alone does not automatically create a refund entitlement or additional fee.

Any applicable fee difference must be disclosed before a change is confirmed.

## 9. How to Request a Refund

Refund requests may be submitted to support@bootcamp.radixminds.in or by calling 9304829718. The Radix Minds team reviews the request and communicates its decision directly to the customer.

Please provide:

1. Student's full name.
2. Enrolment reference.
3. Program and class.
4. Selected learning mode.
5. Registered mobile number or email address.
6. Reason for the request.
7. Relevant payment reference, where applicable.

Radix Minds may request reasonable additional information to verify the enrolment or transaction.

## 10. Refund Processing Time

Once the Radix Minds team approves a refund, it is initiated. The target processing time is 7–10 working days from approval, subject to bank and payment-provider timelines.

The actual time for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the bank, payment provider or original payment method.

If a refund is delayed beyond the communicated period, the student may contact the support team for an update.

## 11. Refund Method

Where possible, refunds will be processed through the original payment method used for the transaction.

Radix Minds may request additional information where required by the payment provider or banking process.

Students should never share OTPs, UPI PINs, banking passwords or card security credentials with anyone claiming to process a refund.

## 12. Non-Transferability

An enrolment is intended for the registered student and should not be transferred to another person without prior written approval from Radix Minds.

Any approved transfer will be subject to verification, availability and applicable administrative requirements.

## 13. Refund Disputes and Complaints

If a student disagrees with a refund decision, they may request a review by contacting the official support team.

The request should include the enrolment reference and the reasons for requesting reconsideration.

All complaints will be reviewed in accordance with applicable law and consumer rights.
