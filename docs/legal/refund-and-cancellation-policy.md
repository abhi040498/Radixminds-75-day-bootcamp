# Refund and Cancellation Policy

Students seeking refund assistance must contact the Radix Minds team within 7 calendar days from the date of successful payment. Contacting the team does not guarantee refund approval. All requests are subject to review by Radix Minds management. The management team will determine eligibility and any approved refund amount on a case-by-case basis, subject to applicable laws and the published terms and conditions.
